## 3. LinkedIn Post (Long-form / No character limit)

🛡️ Excited to share our submission for the @TigerGraphDB HHGOA Agentic Fraud Investigation Hackathon: **CaseGuard**!

Traditional automated fraud prevention systems suffer from a binary fallacy: attempting to label complex transactions as simply "fraud" or "not fraud." In reality, when a model produces an ambiguous risk score, what you need is an investigator that knows what it doesn't know.

We built **CaseGuard**—an autonomous agent powered by TigerGraph, native GSQL queries, and a cyclic LangGraph state machine.

Key highlights:
⚡ **GSQL-Powered Graph Analytics**: Real-time graph traversal detecting device rings, velocity spikes, and pass-through mule chains directly inside TigerGraph.
🎯 **Uncertainty-Gated NBA Engine**: Evaluates quantitative confidence and dynamically triggers policy-approved evidence gathering (step-up auth, customer verification) before escalating or locking accounts.
📊 **Next-Best-Action (Before & After)**: Explicit before/after tracking as new signals arrive—fulfilling mandatory hackathon evaluation criteria.
⚖️ **Hardcoded Permission Guardrails**: Non-invasive steps auto-execute; critical blocks and SAR filings route through a human-in-the-loop analyst dashboard.
🧠 **Graph Case Memory**: Writes closed investigations back into TigerGraph for continuous case-to-case learning.

Check out our full architecture and open-source repo! 🚀
